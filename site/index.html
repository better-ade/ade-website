How it works

Ticket to Done.

  1. Ticket

    A small story with a risk level.

  2. Build

    A failing test first, then the code.

  3. Review

    Scaled to the risk.

  4. Verified

    The reviewer, never the builder, runs the tests.

  5. Done

    Green only. Red goes back to the builder.

What works today

Merged and working.
