# W-hero-keys: the hero drawing shows the keys locked

risk: low (a drawing on the public site; no secret, sign-in, data or money)

## Story
As someone landing on the home page, I want the hero drawing to show that the keys stay locked while the agents work,
so secure storage is visible before I scroll to its section.

## Acceptance criteria (tests/siteHero.test.ts)
1. A still tag above the terminal, a lock and "keys locked", in violet, apart from the ticket and the team, inside the
   drawing's size and name budgets, and named in the drawing's accessible name.
